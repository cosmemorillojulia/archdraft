# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- `@archdraft/web`: a proposal of the assistant is shown and applied with the whole diagram laid out again (Reorder the whole diagram on), unless it removes components, which keeps the layout so that what goes is shown where it was.
- `@archdraft/web`: the legend that says a proposal is only a preview sits above the prompt (above the panel on phones) instead of at the top of the canvas, where the diagram card covered it.
- `@archdraft/web`: connections go round the boxes in their way instead of through them. Right-angled lines follow the shortest route with the fewest turns, down the middle of the gaps between boxes; curves that would cross a box pass round it. Each connection takes the pair of sides with the best route, and connections that share a side of a box spread along it instead of meeting at its middle. Exports to SVG and PNG are drawn the same way.
- `@archdraft/web`: the layout centres a box between the ones it fans out to or gathers from when that is tidier than lining up chains of calls; both placements are tried and the tidier one is kept.
- `@archdraft/web`: connection labels show always or never, as set for each connection in the inspector (Show label) or for all of them with the new shortcut next to the zoom; they are hidden by default and no longer appear on selection. The setting lives in the layout (`edgeLabels`), so it is saved, shared, undone and exported.

- Repository layout (ADR 0021): `apps/web` is now `frontend/`, `packages/core` is now `shared/` and is published in the workspace as `@archdraft/shared`, and the ELK layout of `packages/layout` lives in `frontend/src/layout/`, the only place that uses it. The backend will go in `backend/`. Behaviour is unchanged.
- `@archdraft/shared`, `@archdraft/web`: connections are edited as in draw.io. Drag an end of a selected connection onto another box to reconnect it, by the side whose middle it is let go at or else the one that fits. A right-angled connection has a handle on every part, which moves that part across its direction; a curve shows the points it passes through, which move freely and go with a double click, and a dot in the middle of each part to add another. Routes keep these points (`points` in `edgeRoutes`, in diagram coordinates), which move with a connection whose two ends move together and go when it changes style; routes saved with the older `bend` are still drawn as before.
- `@archdraft/web`: connection points work as in draw.io. Every side of a component or shape shows a dot on hover to drag a connection from, and the connection leaves by that side; let go on a dot of the other node and it arrives by that side, anywhere else on it and the side is chosen. Automatic connections can leave and arrive by any side, so a node behind or above is reached directly instead of the line going round it.

### Added

- `@archdraft/shared`, `@archdraft/web`: seventeen component kinds that software teams use every day: NAT and VPN gateways, workflows, schedulers, data processing, file storage, graph and time series databases, email and push notifications, network firewall, key management, logging, tracing, container registry, CI/CD pipeline and ML platform, with a new Delivery category. Ten templates after reference architectures published by AWS, Google Cloud and Azure, from a scheduled job to multi-region disaster recovery, each citing its source (`docs/modulos/plantillas.md`). The templates menu scrolls when it is taller than the screen.
- `@archdraft/web`: shortcuts taken from Excalidraw and PowerPoint. Alt and a drag leaves the originals and drops copies where it is let go; Shift while dragging keeps the move straight, across or along; Ctrl/⌘ + D duplicates several chosen nodes at once; Ctrl/⌘ + A chooses every node but the groups; Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + an arrow lines the chosen nodes up on that side; Shift + 1 shows the whole diagram and Shift + 2 the selection. The keyboard shortcuts dialog lists them.
- `@archdraft/web`: help and first steps. An empty diagram shows what to do: example descriptions the assistant draws with a click, the templates and the components. A gallery describes the six templates with their cloud and size. A help menu in the header opens the gallery, the keyboard shortcuts (also with `?`) and an about dialog with the privacy policy: diagrams stay in the browser, links keep them after `#`, and the API does not store what the assistant is sent.
- `@archdraft/api`: evaluation set of the agent (`backend/evals/`, ADR 0024): 32 cases in Spanish and English for the five tasks, scored by the kinds of component they require, allow and forbid, the connections they need, the design rules, the ids kept and the goal of each task. `pnpm --filter @archdraft/api eval` runs it against the configured model, or a fake with the ideal answers, and writes a report in JSON and Markdown (`docs/evaluacion.md`).
- `@archdraft/api`, `@archdraft/web`: the assistant shows how it is getting on. Above the prompt a panel tells whether it is thinking, writing, checking or fixing its answer and for how long, shows the end of the reasoning of models that show it (foldable), and lists the components as they are written. `POST /v1/assist` streams `progress` events, then the `result` or the `problem`, to requests that accept `text/event-stream`.

- `@archdraft/web`: the assistant shows what it will apply before it is applied (ADR 0023). The open proposal is drawn on the canvas as it will be: new parts in green, changed ones in amber and removed ones in red, dashed, where they were, each with a label in words, and the rest faded. The panel lists what the assistant decided by itself, the review findings it solves or brings, and each change in words; pointing at a change picks it out on the canvas. An edit that removes a component or touches more than 8 parts is shown first instead of applied. After a change, a summary with Undo stays until it is closed. New parts are placed next to what they connect to, and a large change can tidy the whole diagram.
- `@archdraft/api`: the assistant completes what a request leaves out, takes the usual reading of what is ambiguous, designs the safe version of what is unsafe, and says so in `assumptions`. A valid design that adds an exposed data store, compute without an entry, unencrypted data or a loop of calls is sent back once to fix it. The example in the prompt is the template closest to the request, and nodes are asked for in the order of the flow. `LLM_REASONING_EFFORT` sets how much a reasoning model thinks, `low` by default for `gpt-oss`.
- `@archdraft/web`: diagrams are laid out with fewer crossings and straighter connections.
- `@archdraft/web`: Alt and the arrow keys move the selected box, or the chosen ones, 10 px at a time (1 px with Shift), and holding the key down is one undo step. Ctrl, Cmd or Shift and a drag on the empty canvas draws a box that adds the boxes inside it to the selection.
- `@archdraft/api`: the default Groq model is `openai/gpt-oss-120b`, since Groq shut down `llama-3.3-70b-versatile` for free accounts; answers reserve fewer tokens to fit the 8,000 tokens a minute of its free plan, and provider refusals carry the status and the explanation of the provider in the response and the log.

- Local settings in two files at the root: `.env.defaults` (committed, no secrets: the API address of the editor and the Groq URL) and `.env.secrets` (ignored by git, template in `.env.secrets.example`: the Groq key). `make api` runs the API with Groq without a Cloudflare account; `make api-remote` runs it with Workers AI.

- `@archdraft/web`: the AI assistant. The description bar creates a diagram when the canvas is empty (or with New diagram on) and changes the open one otherwise; the assistant menu asks for improvements, a simpler design or a more complete one, offered in a panel with Apply and Discard. What changes is outlined on the canvas, every change is one undo step, the layout of what stays is kept, and improvements made of edits apply on top of each other. Errors and warnings are worded in English and Spanish. The assistant only appears in builds with `VITE_API_URL`.
- `@archdraft/api` (`backend/`): Cloudflare Worker with Hono. `GET /health` and `POST /v1/assist` run an agent with five typed tasks (`generate`, `edit`, `improve`, `simplify`, `extend`) on Workers AI or any OpenAI-compatible provider such as Groq. Answers are validated as untrusted input and repaired up to twice with the exact errors; failures are `application/problem+json`. Request ids, CORS allow-list, a 64 KB body limit and one JSON log line per call without the prompt. `deploy-api.yml` deploys it once the Cloudflare secrets exist; `make api` runs it locally (ADR 0022).
- `@archdraft/shared`: graph edits as data (`GraphPatchSchema`, `applyPatch`, `diffGraphs`, `hasChanges`) and the contract of `/v1/assist` (`AssistRequestSchema`, `AssistResultSchema`, problem types and warnings), used by both sides.

- `@archdraft/core`, `@archdraft/web`: connections can be drawn as curves or with right angles. A button in the view controls switches the whole diagram, and a selected connection has its own Line field (Auto, Curved or Square) in the inspector. The choice lives in the layout (`edgeStyle`, `edgeStyles`, `ownEdgeStyle`, `resolveEdgeStyle`), so it is saved, shared, undone and exported to SVG and PNG; diagrams without it stay curved.

- `@archdraft/core`, `@archdraft/web`: a selected connection shows dots to route it by hand, in curves and in right angles. The dots at its ends can be dragged to any side of their node, and the one in the middle bends a curve through it or moves the segment that crosses the middle of a right-angled line. A Restore the automatic route button in the inspector takes it all back. The route lives in the layout (`edgeRoutes`, `ownEdgeRoute`), so it is saved, shared, undone and exported to SVG and PNG; connections nobody routed are drawn as before.

- `@archdraft/web`: on desktop the bottom right corner has two panels instead of one: the zoom at the edge, and a column of shortcuts to its left (connection style and flow animation) where new shortcuts will go. On touch screens they stay one panel.

- `@archdraft/web`: the diagram card is wider than tall, in two rows: the title, an arrow that unfolds the description and the cloud chip, fitted to its name with the text centred; then the counts and the design review.
- `@archdraft/web`: the cloud chip opens a menu in the style of the editor instead of the native list of the browser; the inspector and the review keep the width of their column and the description bar ends where they start, so only the diagram card is wider.

- `@archdraft/web`: the header + button is a menu with the blank diagram and the templates; on phones the buttons are closer together so the title no longer covers them.

- `@archdraft/web`: text put over a box is fixed to it (`attachedTo`): it moves, is copied and deleted with the box and can be detached from the inspector.

- `@archdraft/web`: shapes are born without a name, there is a Text kind to write anywhere, every box has bring to front, bring forward, send backward and send to back in the inspector, Ctrl, Cmd or Shift with a click chooses several boxes to move or delete together, and the name and the cloud of the diagram are edited in its card (the name gives the exported file names).
- `@archdraft/core`, `@archdraft/web`: import of Mermaid flowcharts and draw.io files, compressed or not (`importMermaid`, `importDrawio`), from Share, Open or import a file; the `EMPTY_LABEL` validation code.

- `@archdraft/web`: a + button in the header opens an empty diagram (`openBlank`); undo brings the previous diagram back.

### Changed

- `@archdraft/web`: the templates live in a dropdown button in the header instead of a row of pills above the prompt, which leaves more room for the diagram; shapes are drawn exactly as in Excalidraw: crosshair cursor, the live preview is the figure itself, Shift keeps the sides equal, Alt draws from the centre, and what is placed by hand stays where it was put (no nudging away from neighbours, no entrance animation, no view jump).

- `DEFAULT_NODE_SIZE` and `DEFAULT_CONTAINER_SIZE` moved from `@archdraft/layout` to `@archdraft/core`.
- `@archdraft/web`: the header shows the product name in slanted capitals, as a wordmark.
- `@archdraft/web`: every diagram opens fitted whole in the free part of the canvas, on phones too, instead of at a readable minimum zoom.
- `@archdraft/web`: the theme change is a cross-fade made with View Transitions where the browser supports them (ADR 0018), smoother on large diagrams; elsewhere the colour blend remains, and it no longer jumps at the end.
- `CONTAINER_PADDING` moved from `@archdraft/layout` to `@archdraft/core`, so the editor can place components inside groups without loading ELK.
- `@archdraft/web`: the header no longer links to the source code, React Flow's corner link is hidden (it is credited in the README), component names take up to two lines instead of being cut, the palette search ignores accents, and the diagram card says 1 component instead of 1 components.
- `@archdraft/web`: new logo, a small diagram shaped as an A inside drawing-sheet corner marks, as the page icon, the iOS home screen icon and at the top of the README.
- `@archdraft/web`: on touch screens the floating panels are nearly opaque instead of blurred, so the canvas under them is cheaper to redraw.
- `@archdraft/web`: on phones the inspector opens collapsed, as a thin strip with the name and type of the selection and its quick actions (connect, duplicate, delete), so most of the diagram stays in view. Dragging its handle up, or tapping it, expands it to every field; dragging it down collapses it. When it expands over the selected node, the view glides to show the node above it.

### Fixed

- `@archdraft/web`: laying out a diagram with a connection to a node caught in a cycle of parents no longer overflows the stack while comparing the two placements.
- `@archdraft/api`: CI on `main` is green again. The streamed answers of the assistant (progress of the agent, server-sent events of `/v1/assist` and streaming of OpenAI-compatible providers) had no tests, which left the backend below its coverage thresholds.
- `@archdraft/web`: a selected component or figure shows one outline, the one it has on hover, instead of a second accent ring around it.
- `@archdraft/web`: dragging a node re-rendered every node and edge at each step and opened the inspector; it is now about twice as cheap and leaves the selection alone. A resize made with a finger was never saved.

- `@archdraft/web`: arrowheads were hidden inside the target or out of place, because connection points were measured in the middle of the entrance animation; they are now drawn by the edge, with the line ending at their base and the tip on the border.
- `@archdraft/web`: edges were unmounted and drawn in again after every edit or drag step, and looked cut short while a node moved, because React Flow dropped the measured size of each new node object; nodes now carry the size of the layout as measured.
